===== GAME OVERVIEW =====
Players: 2
Goal: Earn the most buttons by the end of the game by building the most complete 9x9 quilt board.


===== COMPONENTS =====
- 1 central time board (circular track with 53 spaces, numbered 0 to 52)
- 2 player quilt boards (9x9 grid each)
- 1 neutral token (small leather piece marker)
- 2 player tokens (placed on the time board)
- ~33 patch tiles (each with a button cost, time cost, and button income)
- Buttons (currency) — each player starts with 5 buttons
- Special 1x1 leather patches (placed on specific time board spaces)


===== SETUP =====
1. Place all patches in a random circle around the time board.
2. Find the smallest patch (the 2x2 patch) and place the neutral token directly to its left.
3. Each player places their token on space 0 of the time board.
4. Each player receives 5 buttons.
5. Randomly determine the first player.


===== TURN STRUCTURE =====
On your turn, you MUST choose one of two actions:

-- Action 1: Advance & Receive Buttons --
- Move your token to the space directly in front of the other player's token (one space ahead of them).
- Receive 1 button for each space you moved.
- The other player then takes their turn.

-- Action 2: Take a Patch --
- You may only choose from the first 3 patches clockwise from the neutral token.
- Pay the patch's button cost to the bank.
- Move your token forward on the time board by the patch's time cost.
- Place the patch on your quilt board (it can be rotated and flipped freely).
- Move the neutral token to the space where the chosen patch was.
- If you pass or land on a leather patch space, take that leather patch immediately and place it on your quilt board.


===== TIME BOARD & TURN ORDER =====
- The player whose token is furthest back always goes next.
- If tokens are on the same space, the player on top goes next.
- Passing over or landing on button income spaces (marked with a button icon): receive buttons equal to your current button income (sum of button icons on all patches placed on your quilt board).
- Passing over or landing on leather patch spaces: take the 1x1 leather patch tile and place it anywhere empty on your quilt board.


===== PLACING PATCHES ON THE QUILT BOARD =====
- Each patch must be placed orthogonally within the 9x9 grid.
- Patches cannot overlap.
- Patches cannot be placed outside the grid.
- Patches can be rotated (90, 180, 270 degrees) and flipped (mirrored).
- Leather patches are 1x1 and always fit anywhere that is empty.


===== SPECIAL TILE — 7x7 BONUS =====
- A special tile is awarded to the first player to completely fill a 7x7 area on their quilt board.
- Worth +7 points at the end of the game.
- Only one player can earn this tile.


===== GAME END =====
- The game ends when both players have reached space 52 (the last space) on the time board.
- Players cannot move past space 52.


===== SCORING =====
Calculate each player's final score:

  Each button remaining          +1 per button
  Each empty square on the board -2 per square
  7x7 bonus tile (if earned)     +7 points

The player with the highest score wins.
Tiebreaker: the player who reached space 52 first wins.


===== KEY CONSTANTS =====
TIME_BOARD_SIZE      = 53
QUILT_BOARD_SIZE     = 9
STARTING_BUTTONS     = 5
ADVANCE_BUTTON_RATE  = 1   (per space moved)
EMPTY_SQUARE_PENALTY = -2
SEVEN_BY_SEVEN_BONUS = 7
PATCH_CHOICES        = 3   (from neutral token)
LEATHER_PATCH_SIZE   = 1   (1x1)
